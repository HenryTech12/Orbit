UniPods Hackathon, official announcement.
The prize is USD 5,000 cash. Your solution will be deployed to solve the challenge.
The challenge: build a chatbot that makes sense of group chats, calls and more, and responds to people directly, so the group stays informed despite the volume.
What to submit: a working chatbot (not just slides or a mockup), access to the source code or repository, and short setup notes so anyone can run and maintain it.
Teams have up to 5 people. Team members cannot all be from the same country. Each team must include at least one woman.
Team declarations were due by close of business on Thursday 17 September 2026. The team lead emails with the subject "UniPods Hackathon" and lists team members and their countries.
The hackathon runs from Friday 18 September to Thursday 24 September 2026.
Judging is done by the whole group. More information on judging will follow.
Name your bot with your team name followed by BOT (for example TEAMNAME BOT). When it is ready, inform +250 783 188 655 and wait for deployment to be announced. Teams should not deploy bots on their own.
No specific technology or platform is required: WhatsApp, Telegram, a web interface or anything suitable.
The bot should help the UniPods community with FAQ and information retrieval.
